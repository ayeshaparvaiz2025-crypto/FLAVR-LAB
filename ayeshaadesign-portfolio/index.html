    How I design

    Six rules behind every post.

    Good design isn't decoration. These are the principles I follow on every brand, so your posts are clear, readable and recognisable.

    Principles

    Clear hierarchy

    One headline, one message, one action. The eye knows where to go first.

    Built on a grid

    Every element sits on a grid, so layouts feel calm and aligned across the whole feed.

    AaAa

    Readable contrast

    Text that's easy to read on a small phone screen, in bright light and in dark mode.

    Consistent spacing

    A fixed spacing scale keeps margins and gaps even, post after post.

    One brand system

    Logo, colours and contact details in the same place every time, so people know it's you.

    Fewer, better colours

    A small palette used with purpose looks more premium than many colours fighting for attention.

    FAQ

    Questions clients ask me.

    Can't find your answer? Send me a message and I'll reply personally.

    Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

    Yes. I work remotely with brands in the USA, UK, UAE, Australia and Pakistan, and I plan calls and delivery around your time zone.

    What do I need to send to get started?

    Your logo, brand colours if you have them, the services or products you want to promote and a few examples of designs you like. If you don't have a brand kit yet, I can build one first.

    Can you design a full month of social media posts?

    Yes. Monthly content packs are my most popular service: a planned set of posts, carousels and stories in one consistent style, delivered in every size you need.

    Which tools do you use?

    Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Canva and CapCut, plus AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Google Flow for faster ideas and visuals.

    Will I get the source files?

    Yes. Every project comes with all source files: editable AI, PSD, SVG or Canva files, plus ready-to-use PNG, JPG and print-ready PDF exports.

    Do you design Amazon and Etsy product images?

    Yes. I design Amazon listing images, infographics and A+ content, Etsy listing photos and mockups, and Etsy digital products such as Canva templates, printable planners and colouring book pages.

    How much does a project cost?

    Every brand needs something different, so I send a custom quote after a short brief. Single posts, monthly packs and brand kits are all available.

    Still have a question?

    Message me on WhatsApp or email and I'll answer within a working day.

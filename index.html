    Lab FAQ

    Can't find your answer? Join the Lab Crew and ask us when we open.

    What is FLAVR LAB?
    A snack-seasoning brand from Pakistan. You shake it on fries, popcorn, potato wedges, nachos, homemade chips and roasted chickpeas. Pick a flavour and run your own experiment.
    What can I put it on?
    Fries, popcorn, potato wedges, nachos, homemade chips and roasted chickpeas. After that, it's your experiment.
    How do I use it?
    Season warm snacks. Start with a light sprinkle, taste, then add more. Reseal the pouch after use.
    Can I mix flavours?
    Yes. Try the Taste Lab to see how a mashup like Smoky Zing or Cheesy Zing tastes.
    Where can I see the ingredients and allergens?
    Open "What's inside" for each flavour in the flavours section. They're also printed on the pack, and the pack label is always the final word.
    What sizes and prices do you have?
    Every flavour comes in a resealable stand-up pouch: 50g for Rs 150 or 100g for Rs 299. The Full Lab Trio gives you one pouch of each flavour: 3 × 50g for Rs 420 or 3 × 100g for Rs 849. Delivery charges are confirmed with you on WhatsApp.
    How do I order?
    Add flavours to your cart, tap Checkout and fill in your delivery details. Your order opens in WhatsApp so we can confirm it with you.
    Are your seasonings halal?
    We only share halal information once it's officially verified, and we won't claim anything until then.
